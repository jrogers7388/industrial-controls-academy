Build the 120V control circuit for a 480V conveyor starter.

Mission: Wire the motor starter

Connect numbered terminals to make a 3-wire START/STOP circuit. STOP and overload must interrupt the contactor coil; the NO auxiliary contact must seal around START only.

This is an isolated logic trainer, not a construction drawing. Real wiring must follow equipment diagrams, applicable codes, lockout/tagout, and qualified-person procedures.
