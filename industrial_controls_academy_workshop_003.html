The Complete Starter Cabinet — virtual wiring, power distribution, and troubleshooting

Your assignment

Build a simulated 480V conveyor starter with a protected 480→120V control transformer, STOP/START circuit, seal-in auxiliary, overload, and 120V contactor coil. Connect terminals, verify your circuit, then measure it with a virtual meter.

This is a simplified isolated learning model, NOT an installation or live-testing diagram. Real transformer and motor branch protection, grounding, wire sizing, disconnecting means, and control-circuit design must follow manufacturer instructions and applicable codes. Never troubleshoot energized grain equipment using this as a field procedure.

    Transformer and protection lesson

    Trace the conceptual supply path: 480V three-phase supply → properly selected disconnect/branch protection → control transformer primary protection → CPT primary → CPT secondary protection → 120V control circuit.

    On a 480V system, a typical control transformer has a 480V primary connected across two phases and a 120V secondary. Primary and secondary overcurrent protection depend on transformer rating, wiring, and applicable rules. The transformer does not make the motor's 480V supply disappear: the motor branch remains a separate three-phase power circuit.
