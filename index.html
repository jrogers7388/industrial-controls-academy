Three-Phase Conveyor Starter

Build the control circuit, test your seal-in wiring, operate the conveyor, then troubleshoot an injected fault. This is a virtual 120V AC control circuit with a simulated 480V three-phase motor power path.

1. Power and components

QF1: appropriately rated motor branch short-circuit/ground-fault protection.
CPT1: 480-to-120V control power transformer with appropriate protection.
M: three-pole contactor with a 120V AC coil.
OL1: overload relay, with an NC auxiliary trip contact.
PB1/PB2: STOP NC and START NO.
M-AUX: NO auxiliary seal-in contact.

Virtual supply: isolated
